# Connected workflow requirements

David requires every change to be checked across shop intake, estimator, shop review/quote, customer acceptance, booking, material picking, fitter job sheet, return visits, sign-off and stored job history.

- Use the same job and room records throughout. Do not create competing copies of estimator agreements.
- Distinguish a customer request from a priced, accepted service.
- Record furniture clearance, uplift and disposal separately; do not assume one implies another.
- Record measured underlay coverage, gripper lengths, beading lengths and door-bar quantities. Keep required coverage separate from whole stock units issued.
- Never distribute whole-job quantities among rooms without a recorded allocation, or silently add spare materials.
- Changes affecting measurements, scope or preparation require shop review. Accepted order and stock quantities must not change merely because survey details were edited.
- Check role isolation and concurrent edits, pricing, booking-message revisions, fitter visibility and completion retention.
- Completed jobs remain stored. Preserve the completion record with materials, rooms, estimator findings, fitter history and the customer sign-off reference.
- Tests must identify simulated messages versus real delivery. Do not touch real jobs or stock for test runs.
