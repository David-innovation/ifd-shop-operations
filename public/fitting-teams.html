Booking rules

Your first-choice qualified team is recommended within 14 days of the requested start. If they cannot fit it in, the next suitable team is offered. Carpet teams: £400 maximum fitting earnings and one hall / stairs / landing per day.

Glue-down LVT: 3 working days below 100m²; 5 days from 100m². Monday–Saturday, excluding Sundays and recorded days off. The fitter reviews preparation, pattern and borders and confirms the days needed. Minimum 2 days. Reserved days cannot be double-booked.

Plywood: initially 2 working days, reviewed by the fitter. Laminate teams: £350 daily target, £450 maximum fitting earnings. Multi-day earnings are spread across reserved days for capacity planning; the fitter reviews the actual workload.

Ask every fitter to mark unavailable days immediately, including regular days off. Forgetting does not automatically allow an agreed customer booking to be moved.

Still to confirm: border-work pricing. Daily job limits and skills must be entered for each team; unknown skills are never recommended.
